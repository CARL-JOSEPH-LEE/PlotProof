Chapter 1 — The letter

Mara has gray eyes. She watches the harbor until the last ferry disappears into the fog.

Elias was born in 1994. The original register has a faded blue stamp next to the date.

Chapter 2 — What the fire took

The brass key was destroyed. The keeper tipped its molten remains into the sea.

Rowan is left-handed. He signs the letter slowly, then folds the paper twice.

Chapter 3 — The repair

The silver key was destroyed. The smith kept every fragment.

The silver key was reforged. It lies whole on the workbench again.

She used the silver key to unlock the door. Inside, a clock is still ticking.

Chapter 4 — A dream

In a dream, Mara has blue eyes. She stands on a ship that casts no shadow.

Chapter 5 — The archive

Elias was born in 1997. He holds his birth certificate under the lamp to read every word.

Chapter 6 — After the storm

Mara has brown eyes. She looks toward the lighthouse and sees someone at the window.

Chapter 7 — The locked room

She used the brass key to unlock the door. The lock turns, and years of dust fall from the hinges.

Chapter 8 — The ending

Rowan is right-handed. He quickly signs the final page and hands it to the waiting courier.

The harbor clock strikes twelve. There are still no lights at sea.
